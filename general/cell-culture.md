Cells read "should" unless they name another word. A dash means the document states no such rule. The 2010, 2011 v2, 2012 RIP, 2017 ChIP and 2017 eCLIP culture sections reuse the 2008 wording with edited values. 2012 RIP gives only its growth-time, stock and passage rules. The 2017 ChIA-PET and 2018 Hi-C protocol documents set only a harvest confluence ([assay protocols](#assay-protocols)).

## Conflicts and gaps

- The culture sections are headed "Required Measurements" (2008) or "Required Measurements and Procedures" (2010, 2011 v2, 2017 ChIP, 2017 eCLIP; "General Required Measurements and Procedures" in 2012 RIP). Most items say "should". The one "must" is the 2010 expression profile.
- 2008 lists mycoplasma testing under "Other Issues to Consider" and freezing under "Freezing Cell Aliquots". Neither sits under "Required Measurements".
- The mycoplasma interval changes. 2008 says "tested at monthly". 2010 says "bi-monthly", which can mean twice a month or every two months. 2011 v2 and both 2017 culture sections say "periodically". 2011 RNA and 2018 Hi-C ask only for results "if conducted".
- 2008 gives a range as the passage maximum ("3-4 passages"). The limit is 4 in 2010 and 2012 RIP. It is 6 in 2011 v2 and both 2017 culture sections.
- 2012 RIP (January 2012) keeps the 2010 values of one month and 4 passages. 2011 v2 (July 2011) had moved to two months and 6 passages.
- 2017 ChIP and 2017 eCLIP disagree. ChIP sets K562 at "7-8 x 10^5" cells/ml and HepG2 at "70-80%". eCLIP keeps 7.5 x 10^5 cells/ml and 75%. ChIP drops HeLa-S3 and adds MCF-7, A549, HEK293 and SK-N-SH. It names no mycoplasma protocol.
- The 2017 ChIA-PET and 2018 Hi-C protocol documents grow cells to about 80% confluence. That is above the 75% HepG2 maximum (2008, 2010, 2011 v2, 2017 eCLIP) and at the top of the 2017 ChIP range of 70-80%.
- No document defines the Tier labels. 2010 describes Tier 3 as "cell lines new to the consortium". The FACS exemption depends on "the guidelines for cell number and cell density". No document states a cell-number guideline.
- After 2010, no document sets a FACS rule for Tier 3 lines.
- The 2008 FACS windows count from different events. Analysis falls "within 2 weeks of the time of harvest". Cells can be prepared "up to two weeks in advance". Fixed samples keep "for up to two weeks".
- The Bionique positive controls use different units: "<10^3 CFU/ml" for the fluorochrome assay and "< 100 CFU" for direct culture. "at least once, preferably 3 passages" does not say what "once" counts.
- Density is "submitted along with any data generated from that culture (see metadata standards)" (2010, 2011 v2, 2017 ChIP, 2017 eCLIP). No metadata list in these documents has a density field. 2017 ChIP has no metadata section.
- "Viability, >90%" (2018 Hi-C) sits in a list of items to record. The document does not say whether it is a pass threshold. No other document sets a viability value.
- 2017 RNA drops the 2011 RNA items for growth time/passage number, density, contaminant tests and frozen aliquots.
- The 2008 example FACS profiles carry no acceptance criteria.
